## 1. What Meta announced, when and where, and how Meta One relates to the earlier Plus plans and Meta Verified

### Takeaway
Meta announced **Meta One** in a newsroom post dated **15 September 2026**. It is one subscription brand covering Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and Meta AI. It takes in the single-app "Plus" plans launched earlier in 2026 (around late May), the paid Meta AI tiers tested from late May, and a new set of creator/business bundles that are built on Meta Verified. Meta Verified is **still on sale and has not yet been formally merged**. Meta says Meta One "builds on" Meta Verified, and according to a third-party guide quoting Meta's FAQ, current Meta Verified subscribers will be told when they can switch.

## 2. Every tier: US monthly price, annual option and features

### Takeaway
There are **three layers**: (a) single-app plans (Instagram Plus $3.99, Facebook Plus $3.99, WhatsApp Plus $2.99); (b) individual AI bundles (**Core $7.99**, **Premium $19.99**); (c) creator/business bundles (**Essential from $14.99, Advanced from $49.99, Expert from $149, Max from $499**). Every official price is **monthly**. I found **no annual plan** in any source.

## 3. Instagram feature-by-tier map (plan, app, price, status)

### Takeaway
On Instagram, **Essential ($14.99)** gives the verified badge, impersonation protection, a 20-link bio page and (per the newsroom and most guides) profile and follow tools. **Advanced ($49.99)** adds the professional set: post and Reel links (4+4 a month), Story scheduling up to 30 days ahead, post/Reel scheduling up to 365 days ahead, analytics export beyond 90 days, competitive insights on 10 accounts, team access for up to 4 people and 1 human-support case a month. **Expert and Max** scale those limits. Meta has said the subscription does **not** in itself increase reach, but some plans include discoverability perks.

## 4. Per account, per person or per business portfolio? Multiple IG accounts or IG + FB? How to buy

### Takeaway
Meta's documents describe a **hybrid model**. Expression and AI benefits are **pooled across every profile linked in one Accounts Center**. Creator/business benefits (badge, links, scheduling and so on) attach to the **specific subscribed profile**, and the business terms say the purchase is **per application or as a bundled package**. Plans are bought in the apps and managed under **Accounts Center > Subscriptions**, and billing is tied to the platform and account used to subscribe. I found no description of buying through Business Suite or a Business portfolio.

### Cited Findings
- "To access subscription benefits across the Meta technologies you use, you should add all your profiles to the same Accounts Center." — [meta.com/meta-one-plans](https://www.meta.com/meta-one-plans/) (search excerpt)
- "Some benefits — like expression features and AI — can apply across all profiles linked to your account, while others apply to a specific subscribed profile." — [meta.com/meta-one-plans](https://www.meta.com/meta-one-plans/) / [Meta Help: Premium](https://www.meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/1864308977565149/) (search excerpt)
- AI usage "is shared across the profiles linked in your Accounts Center." — [Meta Help: Manage AI usage](https://www.meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/1831790234178989/)
- Creator/business terms: Meta One is bought as a "separate purchase per application or as part of a bundled package". — [Meta One for Creators and Businesses Terms](https://www.facebook.com/legal/mocb_terms) (search excerpt)
- Badges appear only on "subscribed and verified profiles", e.g. a Facebook Page or an Instagram Professional profile. — [Meta Help](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/1278609104422515/)
- Billing: "Your subscription is linked to the platform, device type, and account you used to subscribe", so billing must be managed there. Having "more than one active subscription on different accounts" can cause extra charges. — [Meta Help: Billing and payments](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/2534285893740819/); [Meta Help: Troubleshoot payments](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/4472379576332262/)
- Path to manage: Facebook app > Settings > Accounts Center > Subscriptions. Cancel via "Manage Subscription" > "Cancel subscription". — [Meta Help: Billing and payments](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/2534285893740819/) (search summary)
- Premium is billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play depending on device. — [Meta Help: Premium](https://www.meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/1864308977565149/)
- Peruvian press says the plans can be bought from inside the Instagram or Facebook app. — [Andina](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-meta-one-cuanto-cuesta-soles-y-incluye-suscripcion-para-instagram-y-facebook-1092583.aspx)
- Legacy Meta Verified rule, possibly superseded: a bundle could verify up to two profiles (one Instagram, one Facebook) in the same Accounts Center, and plan changes applied to both. — [Instagram Help: Subscribe to Meta Verified for creators](https://help.instagram.com/732454618598400); [Facebook Help](https://www.facebook.com/help/734864594628372)
- Third-party claim: creator/business plans are priced **per profile**, so each Instagram account or Page needs its own subscription. — [AdLibrary](https://adlibrary.com/posts/meta-one-pricing); [Elev8or](https://www.elev8or.io/blog/engage/meta-one-subscription)

### Inferences
- One Instagram professional account and one Facebook Page probably need **two creator/business subscriptions, or a bundle if one is offered**. Two Instagram accounts almost certainly need two subscriptions for profile-level benefits such as links, scheduling and the badge. Consumer AI and expression benefits on Core/Premium follow the person across linked profiles.

### Gaps
- No Meta page found that explicitly states the price for adding a second Instagram account, or whether a single creator/business bundle covers IG + FB + WhatsApp for one brand.
- No information on buying via Meta Business Suite or a Business portfolio, or on web checkout versus in-app checkout for business tiers.

## 7. Usage figures Meta cited, and the exact wording

### Takeaway
Meta's one official figure is "**more than 50 features launched and 15 million subscriptions and trials to-date**". The figure combines paid subscriptions and trials across every Meta One plan, including the single-app Plus plans since May. It is not a count of paying subscribers.
