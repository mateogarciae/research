## Dated inventory of announcements (objective)

### Takeaway
Between May and early October 2026, the main changes for running an Instagram account were these. Meta launched Business Agent for Instagram DMs (June 3). Meta One launched on Sept 15 and moved several pro tools behind paid plans: Story scheduling, links in posts and Reels, longer scheduling windows, exportable and extended insights, competitive insights, and password-free team access. The Edits assistant arrived on Sept 30, US only, free with a daily cap. Free changes included Reels translation languages, Edits bilingual captions, tagged posts on the grid, livestream boosting, the AI-generated profile label, and stricter originality ranking (April 30, as context).

## What Meta/Instagram announced around the Meta One launch (Sept 14–15, 2026) beyond the subscription itself

### Takeaway
The official Meta One post (Sept 15, 2026) ties these account-management features to the creator/business plans, starting at the "Advanced" tier: Story scheduling up to 30 days ahead, links in organic posts and Reels, exportable analytics, deeper audience insights, team access without sharing passwords, more Business Agent capacity, and discovery aids (enhanced profile, bold follow button, automatic follow invitations). Edits Plus (cloud storage plus extra Edits assistant usage) was announced as "soon". The Edits assistant itself shipped free with a daily cap, US only, on Sept 30.

## Free features shipped or announced for professional accounts (May – Oct 8, 2026)

### Takeaway
Free, broadly available changes in the window were incremental. They include tagged posts on the grid, livestream boosting (paid only for the ad spend), the Edits bilingual captions and template updates, First Draft (iOS), more Reels translation languages, the AI-generated profile label, and a Series test. The bigger free changes, the insights redesign, Trial Reels scheduling and originality ranking, landed in April, just before the window. Native scheduling for posts and Reels stays free (30 days per Meta's Help Center), while Story scheduling and in-post links became paid (Meta One).
